# Zack Spanish — YouTube Channel Guide

## Channel Overview
Spanish-dubbed versions of Zack D. Films short-form educational videos. We take viral English-language explainer content and localize it for Spanish-speaking audiences using AI dubbing.

## Content Niche
- **Format**: YouTube Shorts / TikTok (vertical 9:16, ~30-60 seconds)
- **Style**: Fast-paced educational storytelling with cinematic visuals
- **Topics**: History, science, survival stories, mind-blowing facts, "how it works" explainers
- **Tone**: Engaging, wonder-driven, accessible to all ages
- **Source**: Zack D. Films library (animated/3D explainer shorts)

## Target Audience
- Primary: Spanish speakers in Latin America and Spain (18-35)
- Secondary: Spanish learners who want engaging listening practice
- Interests: Curiosidades, ciencia, historia, datos increíbles, animación 3D

## Brand Voice (Spanish)
- Direct, energetic, conversational
- Short punchy sentences optimized for captioned viewing
- Wonder and curiosity as the emotional hook
- Never dry or academic — always feels like "did you know?!"
